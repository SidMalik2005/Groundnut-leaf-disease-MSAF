    Runs on your device

    The trained network runs inside this browser tab with WebAssembly. Your photo is never uploaded anywhere. Each image is scored four times (original plus three flips) and the results are averaged, the same test-time augmentation used for the reported accuracy.

    Where the model looked

    The heatmap is the model's own learned attention pooling, averaged across its three feature scales. This layer replaces global average pooling so that small lesions are not averaged away, and it is the part of the design that targets early leaf spot.
